Operating Systems Revision Notes: Process Scheduling & Deadlocks

1. Process Scheduling Summary
- First-Come, First-Served (FCFS): Non-preemptive, suffers from Convoy Effect.
- Shortest Job First (SJF): Optimal average waiting time. Can be preemptive (SRTF).
- Round Robin (RR): Designed for time-sharing systems. Time quantum size is critical.
- Priority Scheduling: Suffers from Starvation (indefinite blocking). Solution: Aging.

2. Synchronization & Deadlock Prevention
- Mutual Exclusion: Only one process can use a resource at a time.
- Hold and Wait: A process holding resources can request new ones.
- No Preemption: Resources cannot be forcibly taken from a process.
- Circular Wait: A closed chain of processes exists where each process holds resources needed by the next.

Banker's Algorithm (Deadlock Avoidance):
- Checks if resource allocation keeps system in a "Safe State".
- Utilizes Max, Allocation, Available, and Need matrices.
- Need = Max - Allocation.
- Safe sequence is found if all processes can complete one by one with available resources.
